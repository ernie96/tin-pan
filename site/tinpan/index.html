Tin Pan

A desktop app for Suno. Play your songs and playlists, make new ones, and download them, with a menu for every command and a key for the ones you use most.

Download Tin Pan from its latest release on GitHub

Opens the latest release on GitHub. Not made by or affiliated with Suno.

What it does

Your library

Your songs and playlists in one list each, with search, filters and sorting. Like, rename, trash, share, add to playlists and reorder them.

Making songs

Create songs from a description or your own lyrics, then extend, cover, remaster, crop, fade, reverse or change their speed. Personas, custom models and voice personas from your own singing.

Finding music

Trending, Explore, songs from people you follow, contests, a creator's songs, and a search across all of Suno. Paste a Suno link someone sent you and the song opens.

Listening

Songs play in the app and start at once when fetched ahead. Queue songs with Up next, have timed lyrics read aloud as they are sung, and switch between two audio outputs.

Downloads

MP3, WAV (Pro and Premier plans), video, lyrics, timed lyrics and cover art, one song or a whole list. An optional clean-up removes the haze Suno's encoder leaves.

Earning credits

Listen for Credits: rate pairs of songs for Suno in a window that plays each one and asks Suno's questions about them.

How it works with your screen reader

Tin Pan uses each system's own controls, so your screen reader reads its lists, menus and forms as it reads any other app. Messages such as "Playing" or "Downloaded" are spoken through your screen reader: NVDA and others on Windows, VoiceOver on Mac, Orca on Linux. In Settings you choose whether you hear everything, only what you ask for, or only errors.

  • F1 says where you are and the keys that matter there. Ctrl+F1 lists every command; Enter runs one.
  • Every command is in the menu bar with its key, and in the Applications key menu of a song.
  • English, Romanian, Ukrainian and Spanish.

A few keys

On a Mac, use Command where these say Ctrl.

Keys and what they do
KeyWhat it does
F1Help for where you are
Ctrl+NMake a song
Enter / F8Play / pause or resume
Ctrl+DDownload the selected song
Ctrl+Shift+ESearch all of Suno
Ctrl+GOpen a Suno link someone shared
Ctrl+Shift+AAdd the song to Up next
Ctrl+Shift+BReport a problem

Which file to download

You need a Suno account; Tin Pan signs in to it in a Suno sign-in window. The latest release on GitHub has one file for each system.

Windows

TinPan.exe

One file, nothing to install. Signing in needs the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, which Windows 10 and 11 usually have. Tin Pan offers its own updates.

Mac

TinPan.dmg

Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later. The app isn't signed by Apple yet, so macOS warns the first time you open it. The Mac version is new and has had little testing.

Linux

TinPan.AppImage

64-bit Intel or AMD. Needs GTK 3 and WebKitGTK 4.1 from your system. Make the file executable, then run it. The Linux version is new and has had little testing.
